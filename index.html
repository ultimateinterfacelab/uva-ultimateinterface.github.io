Ultimate Interface Lab

Department of Computer Science and Engineering, UNIST

We pursue the vision of the Ultimate Interface: an intelligent, physical, and adaptive user interface that takes the form and interaction mode best suited to the task, the user's situation, and their abilities.
We work toward this vision by designing new interaction paradigms, developing interaction technologies, and building interactive systems spanning both software and hardware across diverse contexts.

We are looking for a postdoctoral researcher interested in building the future of Human-AI interaction. The position is funded through the INNOCORE program, which offers a competitive salary and benefits, with an expected start in fall 2026.
To apply, please send your CV and a brief description of your research interests to Seongkook. Applicants should have at least one first-author publication at CHI or UIST.
