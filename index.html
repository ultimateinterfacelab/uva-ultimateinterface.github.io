Ultimate Interface Lab

Computer Science and Engineering, UNIST

We pursue the vision of the Ultimate Interface: an intelligent, physical, and adaptive user interface that takes the form and interaction mode best suited to the task, the user’s situation, and their abilities. We work toward this vision by designing new interaction paradigms, developing interaction technologies, and building interactive systems spanning both software and hardware across diverse contexts.
